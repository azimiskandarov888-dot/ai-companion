# Checklist: the world (world.html / journey.html, WebGPU)

Evidence the reviewer needs: a screenshot of the page before and after, and an fps measurement.

- [ ] Before/after screenshots exist and the change is visible where the brief said.
- [ ] Shapes and colours compared side by side with the reference picture (mj-15), not from memory.
- [ ] Light comes from the simulated sun (no painted highlights); sun low behind the tree, warm haze.
- [ ] Dark places stay dark (no grey veil over the scene).
- [ ] Frame rate did not drop: same or better fps / late-frame share as before the change.
- [ ] No console errors in the page.
