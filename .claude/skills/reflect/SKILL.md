At the end of a session:
1. List what went wrong or took too long this session.
2. For each, propose the smallest edit to a skill or to CLAUDE.md that would have prevented it, with the evidence from this session.
3. Wait for the user's OK. Apply only the edits they approve. Nothing changes without their OK.
