# Prompt for Claude design

Copy everything below the line into Claude design. Attach `DESIGN.md` (the full
brief) and the inspiration screenshots alongside it if you can.

---

You are a senior product & motion designer. Design the look of **Bob**, a
voice-companion app for a **87-year-old Russian great-grandad** who is lonely.
The full brief is in the attached `DESIGN.md` — read it. Key points:

- **Voice-only.** He never reads, types, or taps. The screen is **ambient** — a
  calm, living thing to rest his eyes on, not a UI. There are **no controls and
  almost no text**. The whole screen is essentially **one living presence**.
- **Feeling:** warm and alive — a calm, living glow in a dark room. Cozy,
  gentle, clearly awake and glad to see him. Premium and crafted, never clinical
  or techy.
- **The core element:** a single **glowing orb** — its own thing, **not a face,
  not a copy of Siri**. **Olive / nature green** at heart with **warm amber
  accents**, soft gradients, a gentle bloom, slightly organic. It **breathes**
  and drifts even at rest.
- **On a dark, near-black (or deep green-black) background**, so it glows.
- **States** (shown through color/glow/motion, no words): **resting** (slow calm
  breathing) · **listening** while he speaks (warmly brightens/ripples,
  attentive) · **thinking** (slow inward swirl, unhurried) · **speaking** (pulses
  and flows in time with Bob's voice).
- **Motion:** natural, physical, **spring-based easing** with gentle overshoot —
  **never linear, robotic, jerky, or fast**. Calm and slow (elderly, often at
  night). Light moves within the orb like breath or water. Respect Reduce Motion.
- **The floating indicator** (when minimized over other apps): it **morphs by
  position** — a small **glowing orb** at a side/corner, a **thin glowing line**
  across the **top** (like a call bar), morphing smoothly as it's dragged between
  them.
- **Accessibility:** large, calm, high-contrast where it matters, no flashing, no
  harsh or sudden motion, gentle at night.
- **Inspiration (do NOT copy):** *How We Feel* (glowing gradient ring, elegant
  serif, dark bg, living presence) and *Apple Fitness* (dark surfaces, glowing
  rings, rounded pills, premium feel). Take the glow, the living ring, the dark
  calm, the craft — but make it one warm **olive-green** living orb, not a
  rainbow and not a dashboard.
- **Buildable:** it will be built in **SwiftUI (iOS 18+)** — keep effects within
  reach of gradients, blurs, glows, shapes, Canvas, TimelineView, and springs.

**Deliver TWO distinct visual directions** to choose from — for example:
- **Variant A:** a soft **solid glowing orb** (a breathing gradient sphere).
- **Variant B:** a luminous **open ring / halo** (an open ring that flows).
*(or your own two takes.)*

For **each** variant, show the **resting screen**, the four **states** (resting /
listening / thinking / speaking), and the **morphing floating indicator** (orb ↔
top line). Present them side by side so they're easy to compare, with a one-line
rationale for each. Then ask me which direction to refine.
