Sunlight Through Leaves

The color system for the app. Both of your instincts, in the relationship they have in nature: green is the world — grass, leaves, moss — and gold is the light falling on it — sun and flowers. Green covers surfaces and calm; gold is warmth, life, and everything that glows. Never 50/50: mostly deep green shade, with sunlight breaking through.

Deep forest shade, warm sunlight, old paper, and ink.
Nothing pure white. Nothing pure black. Nothing neon. Every color in this system exists outdoors.

Rules that keep it beautiful

Do

  • Let green hold the space and gold arrive as a highlight — sunlight is rare in shade.
  • Keep text linen / sage / lichen on dark, ink / soft ink on paper. No other text colors.
  • Use gold for one action per screen.
  • Scrim every painting before text goes on it.
  • Let the paintings carry the saturation; keep the UI around them quiet.

Don't

  • No pure white text or pure black backgrounds — instantly reads as a tech app.
  • No bright grassy green (#7fae5e and friends) as a large fill — it cheapens everything.
  • No blue, no purple, no gray-blue. Nothing in this app is cold.
  • No gradient buttons, no glassmorphism, no neon glow.
  • No iOS system red/green/blue — use clay, leaf, and sun instead.

If you want a different mood

Two dials, and nothing else needs to move: more sun (push gold 400 → 300 on accents, warm the paintings toward dusk) for a cozier, golden-hour feel, or more shade (drop accents to gold 500/leaf 500, cool the paintings toward morning mist) for something quieter and more serious. Tell me which way and I'll retune the tokens everywhere.
