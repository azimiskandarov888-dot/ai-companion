Bob — Product & Screen Spec

A structural spec of every core screen, for the designer to make beautiful. This is the "what & where," not the final look. The central living orb is designed separately — wherever you see ORB, leave a simple placeholder circle.

What Bob is

A warm voice companion for an 87-year-old Russian great-grandad who is lonely. He talks to it out loud; it answers in a warm voice. There are two very different users:

  • ELDER He only ever sees the Companion screen. Voice-only — he does not read, type, or tap. Everything must be calm, huge, and effortless.
  • FAMILY / CARETAKER Sets Bob up and checks on him. Uses all the other screens, reached by a hidden gesture from the Companion screen (long-press → PIN), so the elder never wanders into settings.

Visual direction (decided with the family)

  • Warm & alive, dark near-black background so things glow. Cozy, premium, never clinical.
  • Olive / nature green with warm amber accents (green = life).
  • Elder-facing (Companion): almost no text, giant, ultra-calm. Family-facing screens: clean, modern, large-and-legible, simple.

  • Elder-facing copy is Russian. Family screens can be Russian or English (say which you used).
  • Built in SwiftUI, iOS 18+ — keep it buildable.

the living orb

Purpose

A calm living presence to rest his eyes on and talk to. No controls, no reading.

On screen

  • The orb, centered, filling the screen (placeholder here).
  • Optional tiny status word, soft/low-contrast: resting / «слушаю» / «думаю» / «говорю».
  • Nothing else. No buttons, no menus, no text to read.

States (orb changes color/glow/motion — no words)

  • Resting · Listening (he speaks) · Thinking · Speaking (Bob's voice).

Interactions

  • He speaks → it responds. Fully hands-free.
  • Hidden: long-press ~2s → PIN → the Family area (screens 2–6). The elder can't reach it by accident.
Docked in a stand, plugged in, always on. Design for a phone that stays on this screen all day/night.

Steps

Purpose

The elder's real-world profile (feeds Bob's memory) + the caretaker account(s).

Elements

  • The elder: name, how he likes to be addressed (ты/вы), optional photo, birthday.
  • Family & close people: a list — name, relation, birthday (Bob can remember/mention these).
  • Doctor / emergency contact (Bob points here; he never gives medical advice).
  • Caretakers: the family account(s) who manage Bob; add another caretaker.
Each field editable. This is care data — calm, private, trustworthy tone.

Fields

Purpose

Connection, voice, and behaviour tuning — for the family only.

Groups

  • Connection — server address + live status (✓ on / ✗ off).
  • Voice — provider & voice + a “listen” button.
  • Conversation feel — the two pauses (long “wait for him,” short “reply quickly”).
  • Wake word “Боб” — a placeholder for later.

Standard iOS-style grouped list is fine here — clean, legible, unfussy.

For the designer

  • Design all six screens as one cohesive set sharing a single visual language.
  • Treat the Companion (1) as the hero — the rest are its calm, legible support system.
  • Leave the orb as a placeholder circle (the family designs it separately).
  • Optimize the elder-facing screen for very old eyes; optimize the family screens for clarity and trust.
