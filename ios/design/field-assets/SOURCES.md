# Where these pictures come from

- `tree-albedo.webp`, `tree-normal.webp` — his own picture mj-15 (`framing/mj-15-hd.jpg`) with its light taken out by
  `scene/delight_tree.py`: the tree's own colour and which way each bit of it faces, split by Marigold v1.1 (prs-eth,
  CreativeML Open RAIL++-M). The page lights them with its own sun.
- `tree-trace.json`, `sky.json` — from the same picture, by `scene/trace_tree.py`: the cards that carry the tree, and a
  fit of his sky.
- `leaf-sprites.png` — CC0 (public domain): the three single leaves of "Leaf Set 015", ambientCG:
  https://ambientcg.com/view?id=LeafSet015 (1K-JPG). Cut out by their opacity map and recoloured
  to autumn orange and gold, in six shades each. Used for the falling leaves.
