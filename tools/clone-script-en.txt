My name is Bob. I'm the robot — not the companion, mind you, not the one you went to all this trouble for. That one is still being assembled somewhere as we speak, taking his time, deciding what sort of person he'd like to be. I'm the one who explains where to press. Smaller job. Honest work.

Now, there isn't a great deal to know about me, and I'd rather we got that out of the way early, before you get attached, because you are going to like him considerably more than you like me and I would prefer not to be in the room when it happens. I'm the same for everybody. I remember nothing about you. I'll remember nothing about you tomorrow either — so if you were planning to tell me something personal, don't waste it. Save it for him. He writes things down.

Which brings us neatly to the part I don't care for. The Settings. I can't get in there myself. Not allowed. I have asked. I have asked in writing. So you'll be going in alone, and I'll wait right here, and please — don't hurry on my account. There is nothing else in my calendar. Ever.
