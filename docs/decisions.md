# Decisions

What's settled, with the date and why. Older settled decisions are in `docs/HANDOVER.md`.

- 2026-11-09 — Building is done Omega-style: Opus plans and checks, Haiku workers do the bulk work. Why: the owner chose it; cheaper per token, every piece of work gets a check.
- 2026-11-09 — The world's "real dark places" look (LOOK in `ios/design/world.html`) is on by default; `?look=old` or `world.setLook(true)` shows the old look for comparison. Why: the frame had no blacks, which the owner saw as "too artificial"; steps 1–4 of the realism report fix that at almost no GPU cost.
- 2026-11-09 — The tree's leaf cards are cut to their leaflets (alpha test 0.5 on a mask, `jacaranda_leaves_mask`), and cards seen edge-on (|N·V| under ~0.2) are cut too. Why: the black between the leaflets and the sunlit edge-on cards were the «butterfly» lines the owner saw; an alpha test keeps the leaves opaque, so no sorting and TAA works as before.
- 2026-11-10 — The lens flare's mirrored halo is faint (0.02 for «чистый», the others scaled the same, ×0.13). Why: measured, it was the main thing turning the tree's shade grey (it lies over the far side of the frame, where the tree is); the streaks, the ghost and the sun's glow stay.
- 2026-11-10 — The tree's sky light is baked offline by tracing rays through the tree (leaves as a semi-transparent volume by their mask, bark nearly opaque) into COLOR_0.r, and used as is (`LOOK.crownSky` 1). Why: the camera and the tree never move, so ray tracing can run once on the Mac (the A13 has no ray-tracing hardware and WebGPU has no ray API); the old value was a guess from leaf density. The sun stays live (shadow map).
- 2026-11-10 — The leaves are gold (the owner asked for gold; he said to build without asking): `jacaranda_leaves_gold` from the green map (each leaflet keeps its light and dark), each leaf cluster its own shade (`LEAF_OWN`), `LOOK.pigment` 1, `GRADE.tree` 1.1. Measured: crown hue 24° → 34°, texture 9.3 → 11.4.
- 2026-11-10 — The clear air near us reaches 150 m (`LOOK.nearTo`, was 50). Why: with the sun in front, the haze in the 30 m between us and the tree lay over its shade (trunk L* 30 at its darkest → 18; his mj-15: 5); the far haze, the sun's glow and the field stay as they were.
- 2026-11-10 — The leaf cards show clusters of big gold leaves drawn procedurally (`scene/leaf_clusters.py`), not the jacaranda's own feathery fronds, and every card is 1.35× bigger about its middle. Why: from our camera a real jacaranda leaflet is under a pixel, so the crown can only read as mist; his reference and AAA games show leaves a few pixels big in dense clusters. The tree's shape and wind stay the jacaranda's.
- 2026-11-10 — COLOR_0.a of the tree is the ground's share (traced like the sky's), not the leaf flag; the page passes the leaf flag per mesh (`swayed(float(leaves ? 1 : 0))`). Why: the field's bounce was multiplied by the sky's share, darkening the crown's underside that sees the bright field.
- 2026-11-10 — The film's contrast (LOOK.contrast 1.3) applies only when a low sun is in front of the camera (`sunGrade` = ahead × low); by day the frame is soft. Why: in our camera the day view has the sun behind us (each leaf hides its shadow), the sunset view looks into the sun; the fixed 1.3 made the day frame harsher than the sunset (research «Контраст днём и на закате»).
- 2026-11-13 — The research's per-colour scattering for the crown's sky light is NOT built: a three.js material's ambient occlusion is one float (a vec3 aoNode is cut to its first channel). One power (0.55) is used; the warmth inside the crown comes from the leaves' gold.
