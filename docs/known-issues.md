# Known issues

Gotchas and their fixes. Older ones are in `docs/HANDOVER.md` and `docs/AUDIT-2026-09.md`.

- `CLAUDE_CODE_SUBAGENT_MODEL_FORCE` comes from a guide and is not confirmed to exist. If built-in Explore/Plan agents still run on Opus, that's why; `builder` and `reviewer` are on Haiku through their own `model: haiku` either way.
- In a hidden browser pane, `?offscreen` leaves the sun and the sky black: @takram builds the atmosphere tables only in `requestIdleCallback`, and the offscreen loop never leaves the page idle. For measuring, load without `?offscreen`, wait a few seconds, then draw by hand.
- The sun drifts by itself every 15 s (SKYLIFE). For a before/after, call `world.setSun(az, el)` before each capture, or the two frames have different suns.
- Agents added to `.claude/agents/` load only when a session starts. In the session that added them, run the same brief on `general-purpose` with `model: haiku`.
- With `?webgl` (WebGL2, how iOS 17–18 draws the world) one vertex shader fails to compile: `'AtmosphereParameters' : syntax error`. three.js r184 leaves @takram's struct declarations out of it, and the `getStructs` patch at the top of `world.html` only helps a vertex stage that has no structs of its own. It was there before the realism change (seen on 889596c's page too). The evening frame still looks whole, so the failing object is probably one only seen at dusk (the stars); not yet fixed.
- A glTF's UVs read a map top-down: every map for a glTF model needs `texture.flipY = false` (three.js's TextureLoader default is `true`). With it on, the tree's leaf cards each showed a mirrored piece of another leaf.
- Measuring in the hidden pane also needs the page's own loop stopped (`bobWorld.pause()`; `measure.mjs` `warm()` does it): at full speed it leaves no idle time either, and the sky stays black. A tab opened while the pane is hidden gets no idle time at all: measure in the pane's first tab.
- Measuring the world: the weather is random per load and clouds dim the sun as they pass, and Bob's light moves round the trunk — both changed the tree's numbers more than the change being measured. Set `world.cloudCover.value = new Vector3(0,0,0)` (a Vector3, never a number: a number breaks the clouds) and `world.bobLight.visible = false`, and `setSun` before each capture.
- `world.setFlare(0)` does not turn the lens flare off — it picks the first style («тёплый», the strongest). To test without a part of it, change `world.FLARES[i]` and call `setFlare(i)` again.
- The tree's source `field-assets/ph/jacaranda_tree.glb` is already scaled (16.5 m) and centred; the page's files are built from it with turn 90 and keep 0.2 (`jacaranda`) / 0.07 (`jacaranda-shadow`): `Blender -b --factory-startup -P scene/import_tree.py -- field-assets/ph/jacaranda_tree.glb field-assets/ph/jacaranda 16.5 90 0.2` (from ios/design). `jacaranda-light` is old and unused.
- An alpha-tested leaf atlas with small leaflets loses half its leaves at a distance: the mips average leaf and gap to ~0.5 and the 0.5 cut drops them at random (the crown looks see-through and speckled). Keep a leaf atlas's clusters dense (≥ 85 % of the card's footprint covered at the 32–128 px mips; `leaf_clusters.py` prints it).
- Normal-mapped leaves with roughness ~0.6 glint white against a low sun (grazing Fresnel): 1 % of the crown became specks. Autumn leaves stay matte (0.95).
